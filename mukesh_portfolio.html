About

I'm a 4th-year BBA LLB (Hons) student at ICFAI University Dehradun, also holding a degree in Psychology from Delhi University. For the past four years I've been writing — not as a hobby, but as a practice.

I write across political commentary, legal analysis, health, technology, and culture — for YouTube creators, digital platforms, and private clients. My legal training and psychology background aren't separate from my writing. They're the reason it works.
